Audio EQ

Both amplifier outputs carry this speaker's channel. Mono plays it full range on both; Bi-amp drives a tweeter and a woofer from the two outputs.

Mute buttons act immediately, even with EQ disabled. Outputs are unmuted after restart.

Linkwitz-Riley: tweeter and woofer sum flat. LR2 needs the tweeter inverted (set for you when you pick it); LR4 and LR8 sum in phase. The crossover runs even with the EQ disabled, and the tweeter output never gets audio without its high-pass. Loudness

Keeps the sound full when you turn it down. At low volume the ear hears less bass and a little less treble; this puts them back, the more the quieter you listen, following the ISO 226 equal-loudness standard. Works with or without the EQ and follows the volume instantly.

Play music at the level you usually call loud and press Use current volume. There is no correction at or above that level; below it the correction grows.

Apply & Save

Apply plays the settings now without saving them (a restart goes back to the saved ones). Apply & Save also saves them to NVS. No restart is needed. A change of the output layout (Mono / Bi-amp, the tweeter's output, slope, polarity, delay) mutes for about 25 ms; EQ bands, levels and the crossover frequency change without a gap.

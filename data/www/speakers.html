Stereo plays on the front pair and the subwoofer only; centre and surrounds stay silent. 5.1 and 7.1 play each channel on its own speaker only: with 5.1 the rear pair is silent, and with 7.1 the rear channels are not heard if there is no rear speaker. Level: lower the louder speakers, 0 dB is the loudest. Delay: play later; 1 m of extra distance to the listener is about 2.9 ms, so delay the closer speakers. The EQ is set on each speaker's own page. A speaker shows up here by itself once it is on the same Wi-Fi as the Gateway.
